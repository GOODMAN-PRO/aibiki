# CHAPTER 1 — CARRIED

LOGLINE: AIBIKI open the bill in the express slot, land the four-second hole perfectly, bomb anyway in front of thirty-one people, and get the only real laugh of the night from the one thing they never wrote.

CONTINUITY IN:
- First chapter. The log is empty; everything here originates.
- Year eight, late winter. AIBIKI formed seven years and nine months ago; formation card dated 12 April.
- Eighth of eight in ticket sales, therefore first on stage, therefore theirs is the express slot.
- Both leads have known the other way of writing *aibiki* since the day she pointed at the menu. Neither has ever said it. Nothing in this chapter changes that.

## COVER NOTES

Center-mic, deep winter: one stage mic on its stand dead centre and drawn full height; both leads at arm's length from it, bodies angled toward the mic and not toward each other; breath in white puffs; background pure white or a sparse halftone spotlight cone, no drawn venue.
Wardrobe is the whole character note — his suit correct (both cuffs buttoned, tight small knot, a gray column with one black tie-stripe), hers the same suit worn wrong (his old jacket, sleeves in a fat double cuff below the elbow, trousers short over white socks and black steel-toe work boots, the black bow tie hanging untied in two flat ribbons, two crossed bobby pins in an X above the right temple, two stacked eye highlights).
Ch1 prop touch: in his free hand, Suga's brown pay envelope, flap up and empty, the figures on it unreadable at cover size; her free hand rests on the chrome buckle of the cargo strap slung across her body. The white gaff-tape cross on the boards is visible between their feet.

## CONTINUITY NOTES

- **Chapter set: late January, year eight** (arc 1 opens late January). SHAKI-SHAKI frost, arch tone dropped to 15%, breath drawn white, the potage machine switched to hot — all January-only grammar. Bill night is a **Monday** (Gakeppuchi runs Friday/Saturday/Monday); the next morning is Tuesday at Marumo, that night is Gaku's Compass shift, and the final page is the Sunday after.
- **The clock:** doors 21:00, curtain 21:30, the junior act MCs the first nine minutes, so **AIBIKI go on at 21:39**. The wall clock on page 1 reads **21:40**; the express lands about ninety seconds in, on page 03. They are **eighth of eight in tickets and therefore first on stage** — the best four seconds in the ward belong by design to whoever nobody came to see. This reverses at ch22.
- **Set performed: THE ELEVATOR.** Frame: Gaku rides to the ninth floor, Komako gets in and presses all nine because "someone's waiting on every floor — they don't know it yet." Vindicated in loop 3: she steps out on six and comes back in as a stranger who was "just standing there." **Tag (split, and it died on the night):** "THEN HOW DO I REACH NINE?" / "YOU DON'T. YOU GET CARRIED THERE." Ad-libbed on the carry: "CAN'T. YOU NEVER PRESSED NINE."
