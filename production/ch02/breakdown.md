# CHAPTER 2 — MISPRINTS

LOGLINE: February, three tickets sold of ten, and a five-hundred-sheet run that streaks the last twenty — so Gaku redesigns the flyer, puts her name first for the first time in seven years, and neither of them ever says one word about it.

CONTINUITY IN:
- Ch1's tag ("THEN HOW DO I REACH NINE?" / "YOU DON'T. YOU GET CARRIED THERE.") was struck through on the pad and never un-struck. It is touched here and it does not resolve.
- Ch1's bill was a Monday; this one is Friday 13 February. Arc 1's single bit-bleed stays locked to ch5 and is NOT spent here. The TROPHY SUIT envelope does not appear (ch1 spent it; never consecutive).
- Address ladder rung 0 holds: "Tokita" / "Hayase" offstage, "Gaku-sensei" / "Hayase-san" onstage, zero given names between the leads.
- They sold 7 of the norma in January for a net of minus ¥2,300. They sell 7 again here, in a worse month, and he notices in two bubbles.
