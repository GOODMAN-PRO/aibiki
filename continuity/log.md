## 2026-08-14 — Chapter 1: CARRIED

- **Chapter set: late January, year eight** (arc 1 opens late January). SHAKI-SHAKI frost, arch tone dropped to 15%, breath drawn white, the potage machine switched to hot — all January-only grammar. Bill night is a **Monday** (Gakeppuchi runs Friday/Saturday/Monday); the next morning is Tuesday at Marumo, that night is Gaku's Compass shift, and the final page is the Sunday after.
- **The clock:** doors 21:00, curtain 21:30, the junior act MCs the first nine minutes, so **AIBIKI go on at 21:39**. The wall clock on page 1 reads **21:40**; the express lands about ninety seconds in, on page 03. They are **eighth of eight in tickets and therefore first on stage** — the best four seconds in the ward belong by design to whoever nobody came to see. This reverses at ch22.
- **Set performed: THE ELEVATOR.** Frame: Gaku rides to the ninth floor, Komako gets in and presses all nine because "someone's waiting on every floor — they don't know it yet." Vindicated in loop 3: she steps out on six and comes back in as a stranger who was "just standing there." **Tag (split, and it died on the night):** "THEN HOW DO I REACH NINE?" / "YOU DON'T. YOU GET CARRIED THERE." Ad-libbed on the carry: "CAN'T. YOU NEVER PRESSED NINE." Gaku struck the tag through on the pad at GRAN PORTO and did not un-strike it; her position is that it was not heard, not dead. **Unresolved into ch2.**
- **The express pause landed clean** for the first time in weeks — buried inside the bit as "nobody talks between floors," so nobody on stage ever mentions the train. Suga's towel stopped moving in the booth during it; neither lead saw. Later, passing the green room: **"The pen wrote the hole."**
- **THE TWO NUMBERS — RULED. Do not conflate them again.** **House = 31**, everybody's guests combined; 12 of those were the MC act's own people and walked out before AIBIKI went on, leaving **19** in the room. **AIBIKI's own sale = 7 of the ten-ticket norma** — a completely different number: liable for ¥10,000, sold ¥7,000, so **−¥3,000**, plus **¥700** drink-back (¥100 x 7 own guests) = **−¥2,300 on the night.** Suga's brown envelope carries the house count and the settlement together: **31** on the flap, **−¥2,300** under it.
- **The envelope word is 次 (NEXT).** Hand-lettered by Suga where the cash should be; the envelope is empty. Reader-visible on page 12 and **never spoken aloud by anyone, in this chapter or any other.** It means credit against their next set: he is betting on their future instead of collecting on their night. Komako's held silence fires here. Both of them saw it. Neither mentioned it. Long fuse: → ch4 → ch18.
- **Day-job money:** Komako, Marumo, a "four," full-size refrigerator carried alone on the strap up four flights, **¥9,800** counted into her hand at 17:00 (the +¥1,200 applies only outside the ward, and Shimo-Hazama is inside). Gaku, Compass, 23:00–08:00, one unpaid hour, eight paid at ¥1,050 = **¥8,400**, said out loud by Komako on page 12; pods ¥3,400, ¥2,900 after 02:00.
- **GRAN PORTO:** booth 12, he sits left and she sits right; two drink bars at **¥299** (¥598 for six hours, and he said **"¥99.67 an hour"** out loud — that is his roughly-once-a-year line, so do not spend it again before ch12); **one** aibiki hamburg at **¥690** with two forks because it is the end of the month, cut in half with the left half pushed two centimetres toward her. The other way of writing the word is not said, not glanced at, not thought about. **Cup tower: seven cups**, one panel at the end of the scene, zero dialogue, and nobody in 55 chapters ever refers to it.
- **TROPHY SUIT envelope (reader only) — the figure is RULED.** Kraft-brown pay envelope, corners gone soft, pencil tally down one side, in a rice-cracker tin under the passenger seat of the 47. It held **¥83,000 before tonight** — that is series.md §5.4's "at chapter 1" figure, and it is the PRE-chapter state. ¥1,000 goes in after this gig, which lost money, which is the entire point of the envelope. **It now holds ¥84,000, and every later chapter counts up from ¥84,000.** Gaku knows nothing until ch52. The gag must not appear in consecutive chapters: **keep it out of ch2.**
- **OTHER PEOPLE'S LAUGHTER (reader only):** A6, black board cover gone round at the corners, elastic, stub pencil, white tape on the front. **Two appearances, which is the cap** — face-down beside his elbow at GRAN PORTO, and written in at 04:50 in the Compass back office, **opened away from the reader; the page is never on-panel, not once in 55 chapters.** One dated line added. Three folded ¥1,000 notes live in the back cover. **No entry count is stated in ch1 and none may be back-filled later** — ch2 owns the number 409, and 409 is FLYERS in the box under his bed, not notebook entries. Two ledgers, different currencies; keep them separate.
- **The offstage carry:** used once, on stage, exiting **stage right** — stage left is a dead wall and there is no crossover, which is the entire mechanical reason the carry always goes right. It got the only real laugh of the night, bigger than anything written. Gaku's position on the record: "it isn't written, it's an exit." Mizobe called it their closer. Long fuse → ch9 ("then it's a bit, not an exit") → ch55.
- **Sankaku Bridge: silent.** The set died and the tag died with it, so the night's tag was not said and they parted without it. Gaku's held silence fires here. **This is the reader's first sight of the ritual and they see it as an absence** — the rule behind it is never explained, here or later.
- **Walk-home marks used:** the dead-arch shutter (last year's AIBIKI flyer visible in the second layer, uncommented, as always); the four-in-a-row with machine 2's SOLD OUT lamp still lit after three years; corn potage **¥130**; the coffee he buys, holds in both hands, never drinks and hands over full without looking — seven years, unnamed, and it stays unnamed; the 47 Steps, iced, **step 12 dry under the streetlight, and they sat on it** because the set died, with the rule left unexplained. They have never once caught the **00:04** last train.
- **Address ladder, rung 0 held, and both forms are now on the page.** Offstage: Gaku says **"Hayase"** once (47 Steps, sitting down), Komako says **"Tokita"** once (booth 12, over the pad). Onstage: **"Gaku-sensei"** twice from her, **"Hayase-san"** twice from him — inside the bit she is more familiar than she ever is outside it and he is more formal, which is the series' thesis rendered as grammar. **Zero given names between the leads, here or before ch36.** THE KNIFE is present, warm, and unflagged: Mizobe says "Komako" and "Gaku" in the green room and nobody reacts to it. Suga calls Gaku **"the pen"** and has still never used his name; Ōgaki says **"sensei"** to both; Higuchi is **"onēsan"** and her tag reads **HIGUCH**; Asai says "Hayase-san"; both leads say "Master" to Suga.
- **Voice budgets spent:** Suga = **seven words on the whole night**, all door-takings or craft ("Thirty-one. Congratulations." / "The pen wrote the hole."). Higuchi = **three fragments**, never a subject and a verb together; she does not get a predicate until ch33. - **Industry beat, exactly one: NIWA HISASHI**, Sakuragawa TV. Row A, asleep with his mouth open through the entire set, so ch11's "the scout who slept through chapter one" holds; awake only for the carry. Polite, accurate, never rude, and he paid. Said **"we"** for the network and **"you"** to Komako alone while Gaku was standing beside her, and nobody in the scene catches it. **His card stayed in his pocket — it is not offered until ch11.** Next appearance ch11.
- **"We're colleagues" seeded — exactly once, which is the cap.** Fired at Niwa in the lobby, flat, in perfect unison, answering "are you two signed together?", which was a contract question. Niwa clarified he meant the agency. Neither lead corrected anything, then or ever.
- **F-1 planted, and the January posting is correct.** Per series.md §6.1, Chūō Television posts the next F-1's poster nationally **within a week of the December final**, so it hits the Dead Arch in the post-final dead season, over the flyers of everyone who just lost — AIBIKI's own included. **This is that wave, not the late-June entry-period wave**; the same poster goes up again in June and that is not a contradiction. Text: ENTRIES OPEN 25 JULY · 10:00 · ¥2,000 · OPEN TO ACTS THROUGH YEAR 10. Gaku's counting tic fired; his aside was three numbers ("we get eight, nine, ten"); Komako arrived afterwards, out of earshot, never looked at the poster, and said **"Three."** Neither of them said what three means.
